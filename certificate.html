International Certification Standards

We recommend gemstones certified by internationally recognised laboratories.

GIA

Gemological Institute of America

The world's most recognized authority for diamond and colored gemstone grading.

  • ✔ Diamond Grading
  • ✔ Colored Gem Reports
  • ✔ International Recognition

IGI

International Gemological Institute

Provides detailed grading reports for diamonds, jewellery and gemstones.

  • ✔ Jewellery Certification
  • ✔ Diamond Reports
  • ✔ Worldwide Acceptance

BIS Hallmark

Bureau of Indian Standards

Official hallmark certification for gold and silver jewellery in India.

  • ✔ Purity Guarantee
  • ✔ Government Standard
  • ✔ Hallmarked Jewellery

GRS

GemResearch Swisslab

International laboratory specializing in ruby, sapphire and emerald reports.

  • ✔ Origin Reports
  • ✔ Treatment Analysis
  • ✔ Premium Gemstones

GII

Gemological Institute of India

Trusted Indian institute providing gemstone and jewellery certification.

  • ✔ Natural Gem Reports
  • ✔ Diamond Grading
  • ✔ Jewellery Certification

SGL

Solitaire Gemological Laboratories

Provides reliable certification for diamonds and fine jewellery.

  • ✔ Solitaire Reports
  • ✔ Diamond Analysis
  • ✔ Jewellery Reports
